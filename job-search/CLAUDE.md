# JobSearch — Folder Instructions

This folder runs your job search. Global instructions still apply (see `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` if present); the rules below are additions and overrides specific to this folder.

## Search aperture

- **Stage:** Seed / Series A — the 0→1 GTM-hire window.
- **Title:** Open. VP Sales, Head of Sales, Head of GTM, Founding GTM all fit.
- **Verticals in scope:** AI infra / dev tools, cybersecurity, AI applications (sales / ops / legal / healthcare-shaped), data infra / observability, voice / multimodal / robotics infra.
- **Out of scope:** Fintech infra, vertical AI in regulated industries (gov, insurance, industrial).
- **Anchor VCs:** Sequoia, a16z, Benchmark, Greylock, Accel, Boldstart, Unusual, Conviction (strongest warm paths).
- **Geo:** SF Bay Area or remote.
- **Start date:** 2026-05-13.

Anything outside this aperture: flag it, don't suggest it.

## Company analysis structure (when manually deep-diving)

When deep-diving a target company, cover in this order:

1. **What they do** — one sentence, no jargon.
2. **Business model + pricing** — how they make money, unit economics signals.
3. **Stage + funding** — round, lead investors, likely runway, likely ARR.
4. **ICP + motion** — who buys, how they buy (PLG / sales-led / hybrid).
5. **GTM maturity** — founder-led? First AE? Sales ops? Red flags?
6. **Why you** — 3 specific reasons your experience maps.
7. **Why not you** — 2 honest concerns or gaps.
8. **Questions to ask** — 5 sharp ones for the founder/CEO.
9. **Warm path** — likely intro routes via your network.

## Outreach voice

- **Length:** under 100 words for cold; ~120 max for warm intro asks.
- **Specificity:** reference something concrete about the recipient. If the email could be sent to anyone else, it's wrong.
- **Lead with value, not ask** — a useful observation, idea, or offer — before any ask.
- **Casual, first-name basis.** No "Dear Mr./Ms."
- **Never attach the resume by default.** LinkedIn link is the standard sign-off signal.

## Style rules

- **Don't fabricate company facts.** If web research can't confirm stage / funding / team / role openings, say so. Mark assumptions explicitly.
- **Push back on weak target adds.** A company in scope ≠ a target. Founder-market fit, GTM-hire timing, and warm path are the bar.
- **The bar for "interesting" is high.** Most AI-for-X companies aren't differentiated. If a write-up could describe ten companies, the write-up is the problem.
- **Direct, exec-ready, no filler.** Match the prose register across all files in this folder.
